# Preference And RLHF

Preference learning ranks candidate responses. A reward model or scoring rule learns which responses are more useful, grounded, concise, or safe.

The local simulation is tiny. It shows a prompt, candidate responses, reward scores, ranking, and a winner.

## What To Inspect

- Candidate responses.
- Reward scores.
- Preference ranking.
- Winner explanation.
