# Failure Museum

The failure museum collects visible examples of model limitations.

Each case should connect the failure to a cause: tokenization, missing context, model-only guessing, arithmetic without tools, factuality without retrieval, or hallucinated support.

## What To Inspect

- Prompt.
- Model-only output.
- Why it fails.
- Better strategy.

## Checkpoint

Explain why failure examples are more useful when they include the cause and a better strategy.
